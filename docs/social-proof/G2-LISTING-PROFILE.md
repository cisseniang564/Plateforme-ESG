### Long Description
ESG Flow is an all-in-one ESG management platform designed for European SMEs and mid-market companies navigating CSRD, carbon accounting, and multi-framework compliance.

**What Makes ESG Flow Different:**

ESG Flow is the most comprehensive ESG platform at its price point, combining 46 modules across 16 regulatory frameworks (CSRD/ESRS, GRI, CDP, TCFD, SFDR, ISSB, SBTi, EU Taxonomy, DPEF, Loi Sapin II, Devoir de Vigilance, LkSG, CSDDD, B Corp, iXBRL) in a single subscription.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Full CSRD/ESRS compliance: 82 ESRS requirements mapped, gap analysis, report builder with PDF/Word/Excel/JSON export
- Carbon accounting: Scope 1, 2 & 3 (15 categories) with ADEME emission factors
- Life Cycle Assessment: 140+ impact factors (ADEME Base Empreinte v23.0), product comparison, eco-design recommendations
- AI-powered: ESG chatbot, anomaly detection, predictive analytics, CSRD narrative generation (GPT-4o-mini)
- 34 native connectors: SAP, Oracle, Workday, Salesforce, Qonto, Microsoft 365, Enedis, and more
- Supply chain ESG: Supplier scoring, due diligence, Devoir de Vigilance compliance
- Decarbonation: SBTi-aligned reduction pathways, 24 prioritized actions with ROI
- Audit-grade security: SHA-256 data integrity, RBAC, 2FA, PostgreSQL RLS, ISAE 3000 compatible audit trail
- 100% French hosting: ISO 27001 datacenter, GDPR native, zero US cloud dependency
- Benchmarking: 15 sector baselines with ADEME/CDP/MSCI/GRESB reference data

**Target Users:**
- RSE/CSR Directors and teams
- CFOs and compliance officers
- Sustainability consultants
- General management
