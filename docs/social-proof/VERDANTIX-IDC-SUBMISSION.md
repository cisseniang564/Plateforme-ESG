### Differentiators (for analyst messaging)
1. **Price-to-feature ratio:** 16 frameworks + 46 modules at 199-499 EUR/month is unmatched. Closest comparable (Greenly) covers 6 frameworks at 3,800-12,000 EUR/year.
2. **Data sovereignty:** 100% French hosting, zero US cloud dependency. Critical for EU regulated entities post-Schrems II.
3. **All-in-one depth:** From data collection to certified report — no need for separate carbon, LCA, supply chain, and reporting tools.
4. **LCA integration:** 142 impact factors with eco-design recommendations built into the ESG workflow. Most competitors treat LCA as a separate product (Greenly LCA Hero, Sphera).
5. **AI-native:** Not a bolt-on. AI chatbot, anomaly detection, CSRD narrative generation, and predictive analytics are core features from day one.
